Delete Your Bond Account

You can delete your Bond account at any time. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Bond app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion

Option 2: Email Request

If you can't access the app, send an email to:

Email: support@bond.app

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include: Your registered phone number

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your profile and personal information are removed within 30 days
  • Your chat messages are anonymized or deleted
  • Your coins and balance will be forfeited
  • Payment records are kept for 7 years (legal requirement)
  • You will not be able to recover your account
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent. All your data, coins, and messages will be permanently lost.

Data Retention

After deletion, we retain only the minimum data required by law:

  • Payment records (7 years — legal requirement)
  • Anonymized analytics data

All other personal data is permanently deleted.

Questions?

If you have questions about account deletion, contact us at support@bond.app.
