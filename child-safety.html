Bond — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Bond is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that endangers minors.

1. Age Requirement

Bond is strictly for users 18 years and older. We do not allow anyone under 18 to create an account or use the app.

  • Phone number verification (OTP) is required to create an account
  • Any account found to be operated by a minor will be immediately terminated
  • We use age verification tools where required by local law

2. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on Bond:

  • Any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Content that sexualizes minors
  • Grooming behavior or attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or exploitation of minors
  • Nudity or sexual content involving anyone under 18
⚠️ Zero Tolerance: Any user found violating these standards will be permanently banned and reported to the relevant authorities.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child safety concerns in-app:

  1. Open the profile of the user
  2. Tap the "Report" button
  3. Select "Child Safety" as the reason
  4. Submit the report

Reports are reviewed within 24 hours by our safety team.

4. Reporting to Authorities

We report all confirmed cases of CSAM to:

  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) — via their CyberTipline
  • Local law enforcement in the relevant jurisdiction
  • Regional authorities as required by law

5. Content Moderation

Bond uses multiple layers of protection:

  • Automated detection — AI-based tools to detect CSAM and grooming behavior
  • Human review — trained moderators review flagged content
  • User reporting — in-app reporting tools for all users
  • Blocking — users can block anyone at any time
  • Chat monitoring — suspicious patterns are flagged for review

6. Cooperation with Law Enforcement

Bond fully cooperates with law enforcement agencies:

  • We respond to valid legal requests for user data
  • We preserve evidence related to CSAM investigations
  • We proactively report CSAM to NCMEC and local authorities

7. Account Termination

Accounts involved in child safety violations will be:

  • Immediately and permanently banned
  • Reported to law enforcement
  • Blocked from creating new accounts (device-level ban)
  • Referred to NCMEC if CSAM is involved

9. Compliance

Bond complies with all relevant child safety laws, including:

  • Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy
  • India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act
  • International laws against CSAM and child exploitation

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update these standards from time to time. Continued use of Bond means you accept the updated standards.
