Bond Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

Bond ("we", "us", "our") operates the Bond mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

  • Phone number (for account verification via OTP)
  • Name, email address, and profile photo
  • Bio, city, and preferences you enter
  • Messages, images, and media you send in chats
  • Payment information (processed securely by Razorpay)

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (model, OS version, unique IDs)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data and crash reports
  • Online status and last-seen timestamps

c) Permissions We Request

  • Camera — for video calls and profile photos
  • Microphone — for voice and video calls
  • Photos/Gallery — to upload images
  • Notifications — for chat and call alerts

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable chat, voice calls, and video calls
  • Process coin purchases and payments
  • Show your profile to other users
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and illegal activity
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Send important notifications about your account

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Other Users: Your name, photo, bio, and online status are visible to other users.
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay processes payments; we never store your card details.
  • Service Providers: Firebase (for authentication), cloud hosting providers, and analytics services.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect user safety.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect your data during transmission. Passwords and authentication tokens are stored securely using encrypted storage.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Profile and personal info are removed within 30 days
  • Chat messages are anonymized or deleted
  • Payment records are kept for 7 years (legal requirement)

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware that a child under 18 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Block or report other users
  • Withdraw consent at any time

9. Blocking & Safety

You can block any user from your profile. Blocked users cannot:

  • Send you messages
  • Call you (voice or video)
  • View your profile

You can also report users for inappropriate behavior. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

10. Cookies & Analytics

The App may use analytics tools (like Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with the App. This data is anonymous and used only to improve our services.

11. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication
  • Razorpay — Payment processing
  • Expo — App framework

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
